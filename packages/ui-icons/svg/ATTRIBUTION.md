# Icon attribution

Every icon in `stroke/` and `bulk/` is derived from the **Hugeicons free set**,
which its authors publish under the MIT licence, except `stroke/querypilot.svg`,
which is our own mark.

- Upstream: <https://github.com/hugeicons/hugeicons>, `icons/`
- Licence: MIT (`LICENSE.md` upstream), reproduced in full below
- The upstream notice scopes MIT to the free icons and the package source; the
  paid icon packs are not used here and are not covered by this notice.

The artwork is unchanged in shape. The build rescales the stroke icons from the
upstream 24 grid onto the 20 grid this package uses, and drops the baked stroke
colour and width so the renderer can supply both per size. `stroke/star-filled.svg`
is the free `star` outline filled, and `stroke/chevron-up-down.svg` stacks the
free `arrow-up-01` and `arrow-down-01` chevrons.
